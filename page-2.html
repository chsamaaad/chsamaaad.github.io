Purple Rain

Introduction

Purple Rain is the sixth studio album by American musician Prince. It was released on June 25, 1984, by Warner Bros. Records as the soundtrack album to the 1984 film of the same name.

Origins

Purple Rain was musically denser than Prince's previous albums, emphasizing full band performances, and multiple layers of guitars, keyboards, electronic synthesizer effects, drum machines, and other instruments. Several tracks of the album were recorded live at the First Avenue nightclub in Minneapolis.

Recording

The album was the first to feature Prince's backing band the Revolution on a studio release. The music on Purple Rain is generally regarded as the most pop-oriented of Prince's career, though a number of elements point toward the more experimental records he would release later in the decade.

Reception and Legacy

Purple Rain became Prince's first album to reach number one on the US Billboard 200. The album spent 24 consecutive weeks atop the Billboard 200 and was present on the chart for a total of 122 weeks. Look for more details in Rolling Stone magazine's list of the 500 Greatest Albums of All Time.
